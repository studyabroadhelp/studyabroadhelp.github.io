Recognition & Validity – Important for Indian Students

Studying MBBS abroad is a serious decision. Recognition and validity of the medical degree are the most important factors Indian students and parents must understand before taking admission.

This page explains, in a simple and honest way, how MBBS degrees from Kyrgyzstan are recognized and what Indian students must do to practice medicine in India.

1️⃣ World Health Organization (WHO)

◉ Medical universities in Kyrgyzstan are listed in the World Directory of Medical Schools (WDOMS), which is maintained by WHO.
◉ WHO listing means the university is officially recognized and allowed to offer medical education.
◉ WHO itself does not give licenses, but WDOMS listing is mandatory for appearing in licensing exams worldwide.
◉ Always verify the university name on the official WDOMS website before admission.

2️⃣ National Medical Commission (NMC) – India

◉ Indian students studying MBBS in Kyrgyzstan must follow NMC’s Foreign Medical Graduate Regulations.
◉ The MBBS course must be of required duration, including internship, as per NMC rules.
◉ Students must study in English medium and complete clinical training in approved hospitals.
◉ Admission without NEET qualification is not valid for practice in India.

3️⃣ FMGE / NExT Examination

◉ After completing MBBS from Kyrgyzstan, Indian students must clear FMGE or NExT (as applicable) to practice in India.
◉ Passing this exam is mandatory to obtain registration from NMC.
◉ The exam tests medical knowledge, not the country of education.
◉ Proper preparation during MBBS is essential for success.

4️⃣ International Validity

◉ MBBS degree from Kyrgyzstan is internationally accepted after clearing respective country licensing exams.
◉ Graduates can explore opportunities in countries like India, UK, USA, Middle East, and others.
◉ Each country has its own licensing rules and exams.
◉ No foreign medical degree gives automatic right to practice anywhere.

⚠️ Important Truth (Must Read)

◉ No foreign MBBS degree guarantees medical license in India without clearing the required exams.
◉ Avoid agents who promise “100% FMGE pass” or “direct license”.
◉ Success depends on student effort, discipline, and preparation.
◉ Honest guidance helps students choose the right path with clear expectations.
