Top Medical Universities in Kyrgyzstan (2026)

All universities listed below are NMC & WHO recognized with English medium instruction.

Osh International Medical University

Most Popular

Located in the historic city of Osh, this university is famous for its massive Indian student community and state-of-the-art simulation centers. It offers a perfect balance of affordable living and high-quality clinical exposure.

  • ✅ Focus: Modern Infrastructure & Clinical Training
  • ✅ Indian Food: Fully functional Indian Mess
Year Tuition Fee (USD) Total (INR approx)
1st Year (Package) $6,500 ₹5,52,500
2nd - 6th Year $3,400/yr ₹2,89,000/yr

Jalal-Abad State University (JASU)

Government

A premier government institution established in 1993. JASU is known for its rigorous academic standards and is a top choice for students who prefer a government-regulated education system.

Year Tuition Fee (USD) Total (INR approx)
1st Year $6,700 ₹5,69,500
2nd - 6th Year $4,200/yr ₹3,57,000/yr

Asian Medical Institute (AsMI)

Located in Kant, just 20km from the capital Bishkek. AsMI is highly rated for its FMGE/NExT coaching integrated into the curriculum, helping Indian students clear licensing exams easily.

Year Tuition Fee (USD) Total (INR approx)
1st Year $6,000 ₹5,10,000
2nd - 6th Year $3,400/yr ₹2,89,000/yr

Kyrgyz State Medical Academy

Oldest Govt

The oldest and most prestigious medical school in Kyrgyzstan (est. 1939). Located in the heart of Bishkek, it offers the highest level of clinical rotation in top government hospitals.

Year Tuition Fee (USD) Total (INR approx)
1st Year $8,650 ₹7,35,000
2nd - 6th Year $4,200/yr ₹3,57,000/yr

International Higher School of Medicine

IHSM is a modern, high-tech campus in Bishkek. It is renowned for its international collaborations and is often the first choice for students looking for a global medical environment.

Year Tuition Fee (USD) Total (INR approx)
1st Year $8,150 ₹6,92,750
2nd - 6th Year $6,000/yr ₹5,10,000/yr
