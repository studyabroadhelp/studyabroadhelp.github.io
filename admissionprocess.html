Your 6-Step Journey

Step 1: Eligibility Check

Confirm you have 50% (General) or 40% (Reserved) in PCB in 12th Standard and a valid NEET qualification score.

Step 2: Document Preparation

Scan your 10th & 12th marksheet, NEET result, and valid Passport. We help you organize the folder for the university.

Step 3: University Application

We submit your application to your chosen university. In 3-7 days, you receive your official Admission Letter.

Step 4: Visa Invitation & Processing

The Ministry of Education in Kyrgyzstan issues an invitation letter. We then apply for your Student Visa at the embassy.

Step 5: Documentation & Travel

Once the visa is stamped, we assist with air tickets and currency exchange. You fly to Kyrgyzstan with a group of other students.

Step 6: Arrival & Classes

Airport pickup, hostel room allocation, and local registration. You begin your medical journey in English medium!
